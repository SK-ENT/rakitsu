Monitors, /healthz and alerts

rakitsu serve --config monitor.yaml starts every monitor itself, under a stable session id (monitor-<id>). /healthz tells a supervisor if they are all fine. The model can queue alerts, and the server sends them to ntfy or a webhook.

The step lists and the two testers are explanation (simulation) built from internal/server/healthz.go, wake_status.go and internal/alert/. The recording at the bottom is a real run.
